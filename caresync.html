02 / The challenge

Care is human.
Admin is complex.

Important information should be easy to find and act on.

Care operations span people, plans, visits, incidents, and documentation. CareSync explores how those workflows can sit within one navigable web product.

The public website introduces the product and its care-management modules. The application source I developed includes authenticated access and dedicated pages for patients, care plans, visit logs, alerts, incidents, staff, and mileage.
